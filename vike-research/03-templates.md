## 2. Onboarding checklist / questionnaire

**Use as:** a Google Form, Notion form, or plain email — free-tier tooling is fine for Vike's current volume (Section 4).

### A. Client-facing questionnaire

**Business & goals**
1. What's the #1 outcome you want from working with Vike in the next 90 days? (e.g., leads, sales, bookings, awareness)
2. Who is your ideal customer? (age, location, what problem they have)
3. What's your current monthly ad spend (or planned budget), separate from Vike's management fee?
4. Any hard deadlines, launches, or seasonal spikes we should plan around?

**Current setup**
5. What marketing tools/platforms are you currently using? (website CMS, email tool, CRM, analytics, etc.)
6. Do you have existing ad accounts running (Meta / Google)? If yes, can we review past performance?
7. Do you have brand guidelines (logo, colors, fonts) or should we work from what's on your site?

**Stakeholders & approvals**
8. Who else, besides you, needs to see or approve creative/campaigns before they go live?
9. What's the best way to reach you day-to-day — email, WhatsApp, or Telegram?
10. How often would you like informal check-ins, on top of the monthly report?

**Assets**
11. Please share (or note where we can find): logo files, product/service photos, past creative, testimonials or case studies, website login (if edits are needed).

### B. Access-collection checklist (send as a step-by-step doc)

Never share your password directly — please grant access using these steps instead:

| Platform | What to grant | How |
|---|---|---|
| Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Ads | Partner/Business Portfolio access, **Advertiser** level | Business Settings → Partners → Add → enter Vike's Business ID: **[insert Business ID]** |
| Google Ads | Manager Account (MCC) link | Admin → Access and Security → Link → accept invite from **[insert Google Ads MCC ID]** |
| Google Analytics (GA4) | Add user, **Editor** level | Admin → Account/Property Access → Add users → **sales@vikemarketing.com** |
| Google Search Console | Add user, **Full** or **Restricted** as needed | Settings → Users and permissions → Add user → **sales@vikemarketing.com** |
| Website / CMS | Editor or Admin access, as scope requires | Invite **sales@vikemarketing.com** as a user/collaborator |
| Social accounts (Instagram/Facebook/LinkedIn/TikTok) | Admin/Manager role, not password | Add as Page/Business Manager admin |

We'll only ever request the access level actually needed for your plan — never full account ownership unless billing/user management is explicitly part of scope.

## 3. Kickoff call agenda

**Length:** 30 minutes for **Launch**, 30–45 for **Scale**, 45–60 for **Dominate** (Section 5). Scale agenda shown below — trim/expand per tier.

> **Vike Marketing — Kickoff Call: [Client Company]**
> **Date/time:** [date] · **Attendees:** Vladyslav (Vike), [client names]
>
> 1. **Intro (5 min)** — quick recap of Vike's approach and how we work together.
> 2. **Goals & success metrics (10 min)** — reconfirm the outcome from the audit/questionnaire: [insert goal]. What does "working" look like in 90 days?
> 3. **Scope on your plan (10 min)** — walk through exactly what's included on **[Plan name]** this month: [list deliverables — e.g., ad management channels, # creative assets, SEO scope, reporting].
> 4. **Roles & approvals (5 min)** — who approves creative/spend changes, and turnaround time expected.
> 5. **Communication & reporting (5 min)** — confirm channel (email for official record + [WhatsApp/Telegram] for day-to-day), monthly report date, and how to reach Vladyslav directly.
> 6. **First 30 days (10 min)** — what happens week by week, first milestone/deliverable date.
> 7. **Q&A (5–10 min)**
>
> **Recap sent by:** within 24 hours of this call, to close it out and prevent scope drift.

## 4. Monthly report outline

**Format:** 1–2 pages, executive-summary-first — proportionate to a $200–750/mo fee, not an 8–12 page enterprise deck (Section 7). Built from free Google Looker Studio (Google Ads/GA4/Search Console) plus a manually-assembled Meta section while client volume is low.

> **[Client Company] — Monthly Report — [Month Year]**
> **Plan:** [Launch / Scale / Dominate] · **Prepared by:** Vladyslav, Vike Marketing
>
> **1. Executive summary (3–5 sentences)**
> The one thing that matters this month. E.g.: "[Channel] delivered [result] against a goal of [target]. [Key action taken]. Recommended focus for next month: [X]."
>
> **2. Goals vs. actuals**
>
> | Metric | Goal | Actual | Change vs. last month |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | [e.g., Leads] | [target] | [actual] | [+/-%] |
> | [e.g., Spend] | [budget] | [actual] | — |
> | [e.g., CPL/CPA] | [target] | [actual] | [+/-%] |

> **3. Channel breakdown** (only channels active on this plan)
> - **Meta Ads:** [spend, results, notable campaign/creative performance]
> - **Google Ads:** [spend, results]
> - **SEO:** [rankings/traffic movement, if in scope]
> - **Social Media Management:** [posts published, engagement, notable content]
>
> **4. Work completed this month**
> - [bullet list of concrete deliverables: campaigns launched, creatives produced, audits run, content posted]
>
> **5. Next month's plan**
> - [2–4 bullets: what changes, what's being tested, what's next]
>
> Questions on anything above — just reply or message on [channel].

Placeholder wording only — do not send to a client until a Ukrainian accountant confirms it, and ideally the client's own AP/accounting team confirms what they expect to see (Section 9 explains why the wording genuinely varies by source and by client country):

> *"[PLACEHOLDER — confirm exact wording with accountant] Reverse charge — Article 196, Council Directive 2006/112/EC. VAT to be accounted for by the recipient. ## 6. Review-request message

**When to send:** within days to ~2 weeks of a clear, visible result — while it's fresh (Section 10). Keep it under ~100 words, personalized to the specific real result, one clear link, one clear ask. **Send only to an actual client after an actual result they achieved — never imply a review exists that hasn't been given, and never write words for them to "confirm."**

### General template

> **Subject:** Quick favor, [Client First Name]?
>
> Hi [Client First Name],
>
> Really glad to see [specific real result — e.g., "the leads coming in from the campaign this month" / "the book selling out"]. Nice milestone.
>
> If you have 2 minutes, would you be open to leaving a short review of working with Vike? It genuinely helps a small, solo-run agency like mine get found by other businesses like yours.
>
> Here's the link: [Google Business Profile review link]
>
> No pressure at all if you'd rather not — and thank you either way for being a great client to work with.
>
> Vladyslav

### Context notes for using this with Vike's real clients (do not send these notes to the client)

- **Junior School (preschool, Lviv):** real result on file — leads generated within the first month. Natural moment to ask would have been shortly after that result was visible; if asking now, reference the ongoing lead flow rather than re-describing the original month-one result as if it just happened.
- **Mary (author, Israel):** real result on file — book sold out within 60 days. If review has not yet been requested, this is a strong, specific, real hook ("your book selling out in 60 days") — use it, do not embellish it further.
- **Alex (construction startup, Kyiv):** real result on file — social media management engagement. Reference the specific, real outcome Alex saw (e.g., posting consistency, engagement growth) rather than a generic "great results" line — ask Vladyslav to confirm the specific number/metric before sending so the message stays accurate.

In all three cases: the message above only ever references what actually happened and asks the client to write their own words — it never puts a quote in their mouth or states a review already exists.

## 7. Upsell message

**Trigger:** a real signal — current plan is producing results or the client has outgrown its scope — not a generic renewal nudge (Section 11). Confirm the exact scope difference between tiers before sending.

### Launch ($200) → Scale ($400)

> **Subject:** What Scale would unlock for [Client Company]
>
> Hi [Client First Name],
>
> Quick note based on how this month went: [specific real result on Launch, e.g., "[metric] came in at [result], above where we started"].
>
> Right now on **Launch**, we're working within [specific scope limit — e.g., "one ad channel / X creative assets/month"]. Given the traction, **Scale** would add [specific concrete additions — e.g., "the second ad channel, more creative volume, deeper campaign strategy"], which is the natural next lever for the result you're after: [client's stated goal].
>
> It's a month-to-month change, no lock-in — happy to hop on a quick call if useful, or just reply here with questions.
>
> Vladyslav

### Scale ($400) → Dominate ($750)

> **Subject:** Where [Client Company] is capped on Scale
>
> Hi [Client First Name],
>
> [Specific real result achieved on Scale, e.g., "[metric/result] this quarter"] — solid trend.
>
> One thing worth flagging: on **Scale**, [specific capped area — e.g., "SEO / automation / creative volume"] is outside current scope. **Dominate** adds [specific concrete additions per plan, e.g., "SEO, marketing automation, and expanded creative production"], which would directly address [specific gap or opportunity you're seeing].
>
> No pressure — just flagging it because the timing looks right given [reason: seasonal push / growth trend / stated goal]. Let me know if you'd like to talk through it.
>
> Vladyslav
